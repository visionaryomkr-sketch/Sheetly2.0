2. Click **Extensions** > **Apps Script** in the top menu bar.
3. Delete any placeholder code in the `Code.gs` window.
4. Copy and paste the entire contents of `Sheetly_WhatsApp_Automation.gs` (provided in your bonus bundle).
5. Click the **Save (💾)** icon and reload your Google Sheet.

7. Click **`⚙️ Configure Business & UPI Details`** once to save your company name & UPI ID.
