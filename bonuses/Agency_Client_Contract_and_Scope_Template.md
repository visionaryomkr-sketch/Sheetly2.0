## 1. ENGAGEMENT & SCOPE OF SERVICES
* **1.1. Statement of Work (SOW):** The Agency agrees to render the digital, technical, marketing, or creative consulting services set forth in **Schedule A** attached hereto. Any subsequent project or add-on shall be documented under an amended SOW.
* **1.2. Independent Contractor Status:** The relationship of the Agency to the Client is strictly that of an independent contractor. Nothing herein shall be construed to create an employer-employee, agency, partnership, or joint venture relationship between the Parties.

---

## 2. FEES, INVOICING & PAYMENT TERMS
* **2.1. Total Project Value:** The total agreed remuneration payable by the Client for the deliverables under Schedule A shall be **INR ₹`[ENTER TOTAL PROJECT AMOUNT]` (+ applicable GST)**.
* **2.2. Milestone Payment Schedule:**
  * **Upfront Advance Deposit: 50%** (₹`[AMOUNT]`) due immediately upon signing before project initiation. Strictly non-refundable.
  * **Midway Milestone: 30%** (₹`[AMOUNT]`) due upon delivery of intermediate preview / wireframes.
  * **Final Handover: 20%** (₹`[AMOUNT]`) due prior to final file handover, live website deployment, or source asset release.
* **2.3. Invoicing & Due Date:** Invoices generated by the Agency are payable within seven (7) calendar days of invoice date ("Due Date"). Invoices will be raised electronically and sent via email/WhatsApp.
* **2.4. Late Payment Fee:** Overdue amounts past seven (7) days shall accrue interest at the rate of 2% per month (or the maximum rate permitted by Indian law) from the due date until paid in full.
* **2.5. Suspension for Non-Payment:** If any invoice remains unpaid for more than ten (10) days post due-date, the Agency reserves the absolute right to suspend all active work, withhold credentials, and pause ad campaigns until cleared.
* **2.6. Taxes & TDS (India):** All fees are exclusive of GST (18%), which shall be added to invoices where applicable. Where the Client deducts Tax Deducted at Source (TDS under Section 194C / 194J of the Income Tax Act), the Client must issue the corresponding Form 16A TDS certificate to the Agency within 15 days of the relevant statutory filing quarter.

---

## 3. REVISION POLICY & FEEDBACK TIMELINES
* **3.1. Revisions Limit:** The project scope includes up to two (2) consolidated rounds of minor revisions per deliverable, provided such feedback is submitted within the Review Period.
* **3.2. 5-Day Feedback Rule:** The Client shall provide comprehensive, consolidated written feedback within five (5) business days of receiving any draft deliverable. If the Client fails to provide feedback within five (5) business days, the submitted milestone deliverable shall be deemed **automatically approved and accepted**.
* **3.3. Definition of Revision:** A "revision" means minor tweaks, styling, font adjustments, or wording changes to the agreed concept. Requesting an entirely new design direction, restructuring pre-approved architecture, or adding new features constitutes Out-of-Scope work under Clause 4.

---

## 4. SCOPE PROTECTION & CHANGE ORDERS (ANTI-SCOPE-CREEP)
* **4.1. Anti-Scope-Creep Clause:** Any modification, addition, feature request, or additional deliverable not explicitly listed in Schedule A shall be classified as "Out of Scope".
* **4.2. Change Order Procedure:** If the Client requests changes or additions outside Schedule A, the Agency will issue a written Change Request Form (**Schedule B**) specifying the scope change, additional cost, and timeline extension. No out-of-scope work will commence until the Client formally approves the Change Order and pays the associated fee.
* **4.3. Standard Hourly / Daily Rate:** Additional out-of-scope work requested without a separate fixed quote will be billed at the Agency's standard hourly rate of **INR ₹`[1,500 - 3,000]`/hour**, billed in 30-minute increments.

---

## 5. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES & STALLED PROJECTS
* **5.1. Materials & Access:** The Client agrees to promptly provide all necessary assets (brand guidelines, logos, copywriting, high-res photos, server credentials, API keys) required for the Agency to perform its duties.
* **5.2. Day-for-Day Timeline Adjustment:** Project delivery deadlines will automatically be extended on a day-for-day basis for any delays caused by the Client's delayed feedback, missing assets, or delayed approvals.
* **5.3. Project Stalling & Reactivation:** If a project is stalled for more than twenty-one (21) calendar days due to Client inaction or delayed feedback, the Agency reserves the right to bill for all work completed to date and place the project on indefinite hold. Resuming a stalled project will incur a 15% reactivation administrative fee.

---

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS & SOURCE CODE
* **6.1. Conditional Transfer of IP:** Upon receipt of 100% full, cleared payment of all agreed fees and expenses, the Agency hereby assigns and transfers to the Client all right, title, and ownership of the final deliverables created specifically for the Client.
* **6.2. Retention of Title Prior to Full Payment:** All intellectual property, working files, Figma source files, codebases, design raw files, and drafts remain the exclusive property of the Agency until final payment is received in full. The Client has no license or permission to use, deploy, or distribute unfinalized deliverables without full payment.
* **6.3. Agency Tools & Pre-existing IP:** The Agency retains all rights in its pre-existing materials, proprietary templates, frameworks, scripts, design libraries, and tools used to create the deliverables.
* **6.4. Portfolio Rights:** The Agency retains the irrevocable, royalty-free right to display the final work, project screenshots, and performance case studies in its marketing materials, social media, and portfolio.

---

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY & NON-DISCLOSURE
* Each Party agrees to keep strictly confidential and not disclose to any third party any proprietary information, client lists, business strategies, financial records, or credentials shared during the course of this engagement.

---

## 8. TERMINATION & KILL FEE
* **8.1. Termination for Convenience:** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by providing seven (7) calendar days' written notice via email to the other Party.
* **8.2. Kill Fee & Payment upon Termination:** In the event of early termination by the Client for convenience:
  * (a) The 50% upfront deposit is strictly non-refundable.
  * (b) The Client shall immediately pay for all billable hours and deliverables completed up to the termination notice date ("Kill Fee").
  * (c) The Agency will deliver only the work products completed and paid for up to that point.

---

## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
* To the maximum extent permitted by Indian law, the Agency's total aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall under no circumstances exceed the total fees actually received by the Agency under the relevant Schedule A in the preceding three (3) months. Neither Party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.
