## 2. THE 18 PRE-LINKED CORE TABS BREAKDOWN (6 PILLARS)

Sheetly 2.0 is divided into 6 operational pillars containing 18 interconnected tabs. When data is typed into one tab, all corresponding tabs update automatically without manual copy-pasting.

### 🏛️ PILLAR 1: EXECUTIVE COMMAND CENTER
#### Tab 1: `🎛️ Dashboard`
* **Purpose:** Real-time business health cockpit. The owner opens this first thing every morning.
* **Key Components:**
  * **Live Revenue Gauges:** Month-to-date collections vs. Monthly Profit Goal.
  * **Today’s Operational Schedule:** Automatically scans today's date and lists all meetings, deliverables, and invoice due dates.
  * **Client Quick Inspector:** Dropdown to select any client; instantly pulls their email, phone, city, active projects, and payment status.
  * **Overdue Payment Alert Box:** High-visibility alerts showing invoices overdue by 7, 15, or 30+ days.
* **How it links:** Auto-pulls from Invoices, Tasks, and CRM tabs.

#### Tab 2: `⚙️ Setup & Master Categories`
* **Purpose:** One-time configuration center for the business.
* **Key Components:** Business Name, Owner Name, GSTIN, Address, Bank Name, Account Number, IFSC, UPI ID, Currency Symbol (₹), and 25+ customizable income/expense categories.
* **How it links:** Feeds your company logo and tax details into all invoices and dropdown menus across all 18 tabs.

### 👥 PILLAR 2: CLIENT RELATIONSHIP MANAGEMENT (CRM)
#### Tab 3: `🤝 Client Management (CRM Directory)`
* **Purpose:** Master database of all leads, active clients, and past clients.
* **Columns Tracked:** Client/Company Name, Business Type, Contact Person, Phone, Email, City/State, Client Priority Tag (🔴 High, 🟠 Medium, 🟡 Low), Payment Terms, and Lifetime Value.
* **How it links:** Names entered here automatically populate invoice and task dropdown menus.

#### Tab 4: `💬 Communication & Activity Log`
* **Purpose:** Historical client conversation tracker to eliminate "where did we leave off?" confusion.
* **Columns Tracked:** Client Dropdown, Date, Contact Mode (WhatsApp, Call, In-Person Meeting, Email), Discussion Summary, and **Next Action Follow-Up Date**.
* **How it links:** Scheduled follow-up dates feed directly into the master Calendar and Dashboard.

### 🧾 PILLAR 3: GST INVOICING & BILLING ENGINE
#### Tab 5: `🧾 Invoices (Master Billing Ledger)`
* **Purpose:** Tracks every single bill sent, payment received, and outstanding balance.
* **Columns Tracked:** 🖨️ Print Checkbox, Auto Invoice #, Client Dropdown, Invoice Date, Due Date, Service Description, Subtotal, Discount %, GST % (18% / 12% / 5% / 0%), Total Tax, Grand Total, Amount Paid, Balance Due, and Status Tag (🟢 Fully Paid, 🟡 Part-Paid, 🔴 Unpaid, ⚪ Cancelled).
* **Tax Calculation:** Handles both Tax-Inclusive and Tax-Exclusive rates with zero manual math.
* **How it links:** Feeds paid amounts into the Income Tracker and overdue balances into the Executive Dashboard alerts.

#### Tab 6: `🖨️ Print - Invoice (1-Click PDF Bill Generator)`

The entire bill auto-populates with client details, itemized line items, CGST/SGST/IGST tax breakdown, total in words, and your Bank/UPI QR details. ## 3. HOW THE INTERCONNECTED AUTOMATION WORKS

1. **Step 1 (Setup):** User enters company name, logo link, GSTIN, and bank details in `⚙️ Setup`.
2. **Step 2 (CRM):** User types client name and phone in `🤝 Client Management`.
3. **Step 3 (Invoicing):** User goes to `🧾 Invoices`, selects the client from dropdown. Name and details auto-fill. User enters fee; 18% GST calculates automatically.
4. **Step 4 (1-Click PDF):** User opens `🖨️ Print - Invoice`, chooses the invoice number, and downloads a clean branded PDF bill.
5. **Step 5 (Calendar & Dashboard):** The invoice due date and project tasks automatically appear on `🗓️ Calendar` and `🎛️ Dashboard` today's schedule without duplicate typing.
6. **Step 6 (P&L):** When invoice is marked "Paid", revenue flows automatically into `🟢 Income` and updates the `📅 Monthly Financial Summary` and `1️⃣2️⃣-Month P&L`.
