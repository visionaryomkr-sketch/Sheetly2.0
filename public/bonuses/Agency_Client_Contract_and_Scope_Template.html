Master Services Agreement (MSA)

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is executed on this [DATE] day of [MONTH], 202[YEAR] ("Effective Date"), by and between:

1. — AND —

2. CLIENT: [CLIENT COMPANY NAME / CLIENT LEGAL ENTITY], having its registered office at [CLIENT REGISTERED ADDRESS, CITY, STATE, PIN], represented by its Authorized Signatory [CLIENT SIGNATORY NAME & TITLE] (hereinafter referred to as the "Client", which expression shall include its successors and permitted assigns).

The Agency and the Client are hereinafter individually referred to as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

1. Engagement & Scope of Services

1.1. Statement of Work (SOW): The Agency agrees to render the digital, technical, marketing, or creative consulting services set forth in Schedule A attached hereto. Any subsequent project or add-on shall be documented under an amended SOW.

1.2. Independent Contractor Status: The relationship of the Agency to the Client is strictly that of an independent contractor. Neither party is an agent, employee, or legal partner of the other.

2. Fees, Invoicing & Payment Terms

2.1. Total Project Value: The agreed compensation for deliverables under Schedule A is INR ₹[TOTAL AMOUNT] (+ applicable GST).

2.2. Milestone Payment Schedule:
• Upfront Advance Deposit: 50% (₹[AMOUNT]) due immediately upon signing before project initiation. Strictly non-refundable.
• Midway Milestone: 30% (₹[AMOUNT]) due upon completion and review of wireframes/draft preview.
• Final Handover: 20% (₹[AMOUNT]) due prior to final source file release, domain deployment, or live hosting handover.

2.3. Invoicing & Due Date: Invoices raised by the Agency are payable within seven (7) calendar days of receipt.

2.4. Late Payment Fee: Any invoice remaining unpaid past the due date shall accrue late interest at the rate of 2% per month (or maximum statutory limit under Indian law) from the due date until full payment is received.

2.5. Suspension of Services: In the event any payment is overdue by more than ten (10) calendar days, the Agency reserves the absolute right to suspend all ongoing work, revoke staging access, and pause active ad campaigns without liability.

2.6. Taxes & TDS (India): All fees quoted are exclusive of GST (18%), which shall be added to invoices where applicable. Where the Client deducts Tax Deducted at Source (TDS under Section 194C / 194J of the Income Tax Act, 1961), the Client shall provide the statutory Form 16A TDS certificate to the Agency within 15 days of the corresponding quarter end.

3. Revisions & Client Feedback Turnaround

3.1. Revision Allocation: The agreed price includes up to two (2) consolidated rounds of minor revisions per deliverable.

3.2. 5-Day Feedback Rule: The Client shall provide consolidated written feedback within five (5) business days of deliverable submission. If no feedback is provided within five (5) business days, the milestone deliverable shall be deemed automatically approved and accepted.

3.3. Definition of Revision: A revision includes color adjustments, text changes, or layout fine-tuning within the approved framework. Redesigning approved concepts or restructuring project architecture constitutes new work subject to Clause 4.

4. Scope Protection & Change Orders (Anti-Scope-Creep)

4.1. Out of Scope Work: Any deliverable, feature, revision round, or integration not expressly enumerated in Schedule A is deemed "Out of Scope".

4.2. Change Order Process: If the Client requests changes outside Schedule A, the Agency will issue a Schedule B (Change Request Order) outlining the additional cost and timeline. Work on out-of-scope requests will commence only after the Client signs Schedule B and pays the required addition.

4.3. Standard Hourly Rate: Ad-hoc work requested by the Client outside the fixed scope will be billed at the Agency's standard rate of INR ₹[1,500 - 3,000]/hour.

5. Client Responsibilities & Stalled Projects

5.1. Assets & Access: The Client shall furnish all logos, brand guidelines, product imagery, credentials, and access credentials necessary for project execution in a timely manner.

5.2. Day-for-Day Timeline Adjustment: Any delay caused by missing Client assets or delayed feedback shall automatically extend the final delivery deadline by an equivalent number of business days.

5.3. Project Stalling & Reactivation Fee: If a project is stalled for more than twenty-one (21) calendar days due to Client unresponsive conduct or pending assets, the Agency shall invoice for all work done to date and place the project on hold. Resuming a stalled project shall incur a 15% reactivation administrative fee.

6. Intellectual Property Rights & Source Files

6.1. Conditional Transfer of Ownership: All intellectual property rights, trademarks, and copyright in final deliverables shall transfer to the Client ONLY AND EXCLUSIVELY upon receipt of 100% full cleared payment of all invoice amounts.

6.2. Title Retention: Prior to 100% payment clearance, all source files (Figma, code repositories, Adobe raw files) remain the sole exclusive property of the Agency.

6.3. Pre-existing Code & Agency Frameworks: The Agency retains full ownership of its pre-existing code libraries, developer tools, algorithms, and design systems used across projects.

6.4. Portfolio Rights: The Agency reserves the right to feature the final project, screenshots, case study metrics, and designs in its portfolio, social media, and award submissions.

7. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

Each Party agrees to treat all proprietary business data, financial metrics, customer lists, and credentials received from the other Party as strictly confidential, exercising the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential assets.

8. Termination & Kill Fee

8.1. Written Notice: Either Party may terminate this Agreement by serving seven (7) calendar days' written notice via email.

8.2. Kill Fee: In the event the Client terminates for convenience prior to completion:
(a) The 50% initial advance deposit is non-refundable.
(b) The Client shall pay for all hours and milestones completed up to the date of termination ("Kill Fee").
(c) The Agency shall deliver only the assets that have been fully paid for.

9. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by Indian law, the Agency's total aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall under no circumstances exceed the total fees actually paid by the Client to the Agency under the applicable SOW. In no event shall either party be liable for indirect, incidental, or lost-profit damages.

10. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall first be resolved through friendly conciliation, failing which it shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The exclusive jurisdiction for court proceedings shall reside with the competent courts at [YOUR CITY, e.g. New Delhi / Mumbai / Bengaluru], India.
